For MCA / ROC filings

GeoStamp India

Turns iPhone photos of your registered office into geo-tagged photos. The location and time printed on each photo come only from the GPS and clock data your phone saved when the photo was taken. Nothing can be typed in or changed here.

HEIC or JPEG straight from the iPhone camera. You can pick several. On a computer you can also drop files here.

Photos stay on this device. Only the coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap to look up the address and map tile.

Why it holds up

  • Latitude, longitude, date and time are read from the photo's own EXIF data. There is no field to edit them.
  • Photos with no GPS or no capture time are refused, never guessed.
  • The saved JPEG keeps the original GPS, capture time, time zone and camera details in its EXIF, so anyone can check the stamp against the file.
  • The file is marked as processed by GeoStamp India, so nothing about the edit is hidden.
  • Keep your original iPhone photos in case the ROC asks for them.

What the ROC looks for

  • Outside view: the building with the company name board clearly readable (name, CIN, address). Asked for in MGT-7 / MGT-7A from FY 2024-25 and in INC-22A.
  • Inside view: the office with at least one director or KMP present, for INC-22A.
  • Take the photos at the registered office itself, in daylight, standing outside for a good GPS fix.
  • No screenshots, filters or edited copies.

Form rules change. Check the attachment instructions of the form on the MCA V3 portal before filing.

No location found?

  1. iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Camera: set While Using and turn on Precise Location. Retake the photo.
  2. iOS can remove location when a photo is picked from the Photo Library in a browser. Instead, in Photos tap Share › Options, turn on Location and All Photos Data, then Save to Files.
  3. Here, tap Choose photos › Browse and pick the copy from Files.
  4. On a computer, AirDrop or copy the originals over USB and choose them here.
